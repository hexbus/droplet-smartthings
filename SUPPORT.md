# Support policy

This is an unofficial, experimental personal project, shared as-is. There is no support service and no commitment to answer questions, investigate reports, accept contributions, maintain compatibility, or publish updates. The project may stop receiving updates at any time.

Read the README, release notes, and known limitations before installing. Samsung and Hydrific do not support this unofficial driver. Do not rely on its readings or routines as your sole means of detecting leaks or preventing water damage.

GitHub issues may be used to leave reproducible bug reports for other users and possible future investigation. Opening an issue does not create a support commitment. Do not include pairing codes, access tokens, account details, or private network information. Include the driver version, hub model/firmware, Droplet firmware, and steps to reproduce instead.

Community members may help each other voluntarily. Please do not contact the author privately for installation help or request emergency assistance through this repository.
